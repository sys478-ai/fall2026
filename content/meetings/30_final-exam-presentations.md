## In This Class

Present your chosen AI case and public-facing explainer or deliberation activity. Compare the proposed system with a credible alternative and explain what feedback changed.

## Mechanism and Society

Show how institutional priorities shape the mechanism and how design redistributes authority, benefits, and burdens. Compare two course lenses and distinguish evidence, values, institutional choices, and uncertainty.

**Student question:** What can you now answer, and what remains unresolved?

**Project connection:** Present the student-selected final; draw on the two short neuromorphic analyses as shared practice.

## Closing Reflection

What should others understand before deciding whether or how to use this system?
