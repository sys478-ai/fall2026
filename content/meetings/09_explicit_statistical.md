## Societal / Ethical Questions

- Who defines the categories?
- When is it better to use learned patterns instead of explicit rules?
- Who is accountable for mistakes?

## Connections to Course Lenses

Social / political and moral lenses help distinguish who defines a category from whether that category and its consequences are justified. Distinguish descriptive, normative, and political / institutional claims, and identify what remains uncertain.
