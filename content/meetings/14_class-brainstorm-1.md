## Societal / Ethical Questions

- What does a hopeful future look like for people, other living beings, and the places we share?
- When we say “AI,” what are we referring to? A particular technology, a set of practices, an industry, or a story about the future?
- How do "AI" technologies, practices, and stories amplify, diminish, or reshape what people do and value?
- Whose values and ways of life do these socio-technical systems, and whose do they marginalize?
- What should we preserve or change as we shape the futures we want?

## Connections to Course Lenses
Given what you know about how these technologies work, how might the various frameworks we have been discussing (biological, psychological, social / political, moral / ethical, and technical) help a visitor notice different dimensions of your issue? 

Distinguish between claims about what is happening and judgments about what should happen. What roles do institutions and power play? What remains uncertain or needs further investigation?
