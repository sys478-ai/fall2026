## In This Class

Exchange materials for student-selected AI cases. Compare an explainer alone with an explainer plus meaningful alternatives; record misunderstandings and disagreements. Classmates test materials, not represent affected publics.

## Mechanism and Society

How mechanisms are explained shapes deliberation; participants’ questions can expose missing evidence, alternatives, and design requirements.

**Student question:** Who decides what AI is for?

**Project connection:** Pilot final-project materials and document what needs revision.

## Closing Reflection

Did understanding translate into an opportunity to influence a decision?
