## In This Class

- **Finish Tuesday.** Pick up any LLM activity we did not complete.
- **Facilitated brainstorm.** Build the symposium from your proposals.
  1. Silent round: each person condenses their idea onto a card.
  2. Cluster: group cards by topic and by what visitors would *do*.
  3. Test: for each cluster, ask the five showcase questions – representation, procedure, priorities, impacts, history.
  4. Vote: choose two or three directions worth developing.
  5. Name the theme: what question could tie the work together?

## Societal / Ethical Questions

- What do visitors need to understand to judge an AI system for themselves?
- Who decides which AI systems get attention, and which get left out?
- What would make a symposium piece an invitation to think rather than a warning or an advertisement?

## Connections to Course Lenses

Use the four lenses to test each idea. What does a biological, psychological, social / political, or moral lens let a visitor notice? Sort claims a piece would make into descriptive, normative, and political / institutional, and mark what remains uncertain.

## Closing Reflection

Which idea from today would you most want to work on, and what would a visitor do at your piece?
