## Today's Agenda

{: .first-col-nowrap}
| Time | Activity | Details |
| -- | -- | -- |
| 10 mins | **Introduce the symposium** | [Overview of the public exhibit format, the five themes, and the core expectations](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RO0D4q9kl8DMpCRJ68HVNG8dENCveRFA9v198ugvguI/edit?tab=t.338dene5vyal): investigate a real system; use evidence; analyze choices and impacts; take a position on what ought to be done; engage visitors |
| 15 mins | **Introduction to the 5 topics** | Read the briefs individually (see left subtabs), identify top three interests, and write down one compelling question about each |
| 20 mins | **Round 1: Investigate a case** | Each table will pick a topic (let's shoot for breadth). Think of your table as your temporary group. Please read one linked case study and discuss: what happened, who was affected, and what you don't yet know but want to learn more about. |
| 20 mins | **Round 2: Investigate another case** | Now pick a different topic and repeat the exercise. This time, identify the technology, institutional incentives, and alternative choices. |
| 5 mins | **Break** | Five-minute break before whole-class discussions |
| 25 mins | **Share findings and sharpen questions** | Each group will share someting they discussed. As a class, we will revise the questions, cases, etc. and identify actual, researchable questions. |
| 5 mins | **By midnight tonight** | Submit this Google Form, ranking your top three topics, identifying a case you'd like to investigate, and submiting any team considerations privately. |
