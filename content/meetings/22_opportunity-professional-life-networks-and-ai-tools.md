## In This Class

Begin with judgments about a chatbot, adaptive robot, and brain-inspired circuit. Add behavior, training, and architecture evidence; revise judgments through all four lenses. Distinguish evidence of experience from reasons for precaution.

## Mechanism and Society

Learning, general capability, neural resemblance, and subjective experience are distinct claims; institutions decide whose evidence counts and how uncertainty affects treatment.

**Student question:** AGI: it can learn and become sentient?

**Project connection:** Add claim → evidence → uncertainty → response to the student-selected final.

## Closing Reflection

What would change your mind, and how should we act while uncertain?
