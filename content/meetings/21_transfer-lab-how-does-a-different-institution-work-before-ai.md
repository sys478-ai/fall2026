## In This Class

Compare an optional AI tutor with mandatory adoption alongside reduced human support. Frame the educational problem three ways and compare AI/non-AI responses; discuss enthusiasm and resentment.

Objectives and interfaces embody definitions of improvement; competing experiences of help, dependence, or displacement shape public attitudes.

**Project connection:** Defend the final project’s problem framing and credible alternatives.
