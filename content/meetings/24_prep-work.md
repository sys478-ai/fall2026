## Today's Agenda

1. **Deadlines and Symposium Logistics (5 mins)**

   - **Thursday during class:** 
      - Practice run of your interactive element + feedback from your classmates. 
      - Come with a final draft of your poster, and ready to do your walkthrough

   - **Thursday at the end of class:** 
      - Submit to Sarah a print-ready PDF (48 × 36 inches, landscape). I will review your posters and ask for any feedback if needed. 

   - **Friday, November 13, in the morning**: Sarah sends posters off for printing at the libray.

   - **[Symposium 5 assignment](../assignments/symposium-05):** Friday, November 13, at 11:59 PM.

   - **AI & Society Symposium:** Tuesday, November 17, 2:30–4:30 PM.
      - **Attendance is mandatory.** All team members must participate in the symposium.

2. **Review Canvas Feedback (10 mins)**

   - Open Canvas review your team's Symposium 4 submission from Friday and feedback.
   - Discuss whether your work addresses all the exhibit requirements:
     - Overarching argument
     - What You See
     - What's Underneath
     - What's at Stake
     - What Could Be Different
     - Visitor takeaway
   - Identify any gaps or questions.

3. **Share out with the class (15 mins)**

   Prepare a share out your current status to the rest of the class (low stakes, informal), explaining:
   - Your central argument and the larger issue about AI and society it illustrates.
   - What visitors will see, do, and learn.
   - How your interactive element will work (show a prototype or demo if available).
   - What you want visitors to take away.
   - Next steps and TODOs

4. **Team Presentations and Q&A (25 mins)**

   Each team has:
   - **3 minutes** to present its exhibit plan.
   - **3 minutes** for questions and feedback from classmates.

   While listening, complete the peer feedback form for each team.

5. **Team Debrief and Planning (15 mins)**

   - Review feedback from classmates.
   - Identify your 2–3 highest-priority revisions.
   - Complete the [team debrief form](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Cpgz4vMJTNRxGCYvinQd-dfFscPu7G-BQGzgHJm1HC8/edit?usp=sharing) - just for internal use...you don't need to turn it in.
   - Assign a responsible person and deadline for each outstanding task.

6. **Work Time (30 mins)**

   - Focus on completing your poster for Friday morning's printing deadline.
   - Continue developing and testing your interactive element as time allows.
