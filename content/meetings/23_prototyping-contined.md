## Today's Task

1. **Meet with your team (20 mins).** Talk through the current status of your poster outline and interactive element. What have you completed? What still needs work? What decisions do you need to make?

1. **Pair with another group (30 mins).** Walk them through your planned exhibit, including your central argument, supporting evidence, poster, and interactive element. Give each other feedback:

    - Is the central argument clear?
    - Does the poster explain the larger issues and support its claims with evidence?
    - Does the interactive element help visitors understand the argument?
    - What's confusing, missing, or underdeveloped?

1. **Continue building your exhibit (35 mins).** Use the feedback to revise your poster and interactive element. Focus on developing actual content, including your systems diagram, research findings, visuals, and citations.

1. **Make a plan for your next steps:**
    - Due Friday: [Symposium 4 Submission](../assignments/symposium-04)
    - Due Tuesday (November 10): Your team should have a complete first draft of your poster and a working version of your interactive element. 
        - Identify what still needs to be done, divide responsibilities, and agree on deadlines.
        - Be prepared to share out again. Your goal is to have a complete, testable exhibit by Tuesday. It doesn't need to be polished, but the research, argument, and major components should be in place.
