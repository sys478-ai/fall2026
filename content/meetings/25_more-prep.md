## Today's Agenda

> **Reminders:**
> - **Poster deadline:** Submit a print-ready PDF (48 × 36 inches, landscape) by the ened of class.
> - **Symposium 5 assignment:** Friday, November 13, at 11:59 PM.
> - **AI & Society Symposium:** Tuesday, November 17, 2:30–4:30 PM. Attendance is mandatory.

1. **Prep for Trial Run (15 mins)**

   - Get your poster draft and interactive element ready to share.
   - Decide how you'll guide visitors through your five-minute exhibit.
   - Identify any specific questions you'd like feedback on.

2. **Trial Run and Team Feedback (40 mins)**

   - Pair with another team and take turns walking through your exhibits.
   - Run your complete five-minute visitor experience as realistically as possible.
   - Use the peer feedback form to provide constructive feedback:
     - What is the main takeaway?
     - What was confusing or missing?
     - Does the interactive element support the argument?
   - Discuss what worked and what needs improvement.

3. **Group Work and Final Edits (45 mins)**

   - Review feedback and prioritize final revisions.
   - Finalize poster content, evidence, citations, and layout.
   - Test and troubleshoot your interactive element.
   - Confirm team roles, equipment needs, and symposium logistics.
   - Assign responsibilities for any remaining tasks.
