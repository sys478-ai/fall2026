> ### Consider the following scenarios:
> * Removing your hand from a hot stove
> * Doing a double-twisting backflip
> * Getting sick after eating a particular food
> * Getting nervous before giving a speech
> * Feeling afraid of dogs
> * Cracking a joke
> * Crying at the end of a bad day
> * Recalling what you did on a particular day 3 years ago

### Discuss
For each of the examples above, discuss these questions:

* Did learning occur? Why or why not?
* What changed as a result of experience?
* Did the change happen through repetition, a single event, or not through learning at all?
* Does the person need to be consciously aware of the change?
* What kind of learning might this represent?

### Compare the examples
* Which examples involve learning a skill?
* Which involve forming an association?
* Which involve memory for an event?
* Which involve an automatic response rather than learning?
* What do these examples suggest about whether “learning” is one biological process or many?
