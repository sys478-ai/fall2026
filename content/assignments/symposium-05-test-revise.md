## What You're Doing

Present your nearly complete exhibit to classmates. Observe how people respond, where they get confused, and whether your interactive element helps communicate your argument.

## What To Submit

1. **A near-final exhibit.**
1. **A five-minute practice presentation.**
1. **Feedback from at least two peers or groups.**
1. **Three concrete revisions** you will make before the symposium.

Submit one package per team on Canvas by the due date shown above.

### Tips
- You do not have to act on every piece of feedback. Decide what to change and what to keep, and be able to say why.
- After the symposium, each of you will submit an individual reflection.
