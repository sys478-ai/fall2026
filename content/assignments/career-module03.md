## What To Submit

After class, submit a short reflection, about 300-500 words, that includes:

1. One thing you learned from the cost-of-living activity.
1. One salary or occupation detail you found through research.
1. How your anticipated budget compares with one possible career path.
1. A starting salary range you think may be realistic for one possible path.
1. One next step you want to take with this information.
1. Any update you want to make to your SMART goal.
