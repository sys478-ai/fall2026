## What is our goal?
- What do we want people to **understand, question, or experience** at the showcase?
- What kinds of AI systems, controversies, or questions feel most worth exploring?
- What would make this feel meaningful rather than like “just another class assignment”?

## Format
- What would make an exhibit, poster, demo, activity, or other piece interesting enough that someone would stop and engage with it?
What could visitors **do**, rather than just read?
What theme or question could tie all of the work together so the showcase feels like one project rather than unrelated pieces?

## General Approach
Whatever topic you choose, your showcase piece should creatively help visitors understand what is happening beneath the surface.

It should engage with these questions:

1. **Representation:** What does the system receive, and how is the world turned into data?
2. **Procedure:** What does the system actually do with that information?
3. **Priorities:** What is it trying to get right, and who decided what counts as success?
4. **Impacts:** What changes when the system is used – and for whom?
5. **History:** How is this similar to technologies, systems, or controversies that came before? What is genuinely new?

> **And across all five, keep asking:**
> 
> How are we deciding what should happen?
> 
> The goal is not just to explain these questions in text. Think about how a visitor could see, experience, manipulate, compare, or question them.
