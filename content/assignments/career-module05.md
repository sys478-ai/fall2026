## Homework: Big Interview Practice

Create your Big Interview account and complete the SYS Interview practice assignment. Use the [Big Interview instructions](/fall2026/documents/module05/Big%20Interview%20Assignment%20Instructions.docx). Your Big Interview practice session is due in 2 weeks.

Big Interview is overseen by the Career Center. If you have trouble accessing it, email career@unca.edu or visit the Career Center in Ramsey Library.

## What To Submit

After you complete the homework, submit your Big Interview practice assignment and a short reflection, about 300-500 words, that includes:

1. One career story you drafted.
1. One thing you learned from practicing "Tell me about yourself."
1. One part of your interview answer you want to keep improving.
1. A brief update on your SMART goal.
