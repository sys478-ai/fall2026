Think of an example of something you have learned and explain it through two lenses, then consider one ethical question it raises.

> **Goal:** Practice seeing the same situation in more than one way. Each lens reveals something and leaves something out.

## Choose an Example

Use an example from your life or everyday life, such as:

* learning a skill
* building a habit
* picking up a social norm
* changing your mind
* learning from feedback, failure, or another person

## Choose Two Lenses

Pick the two lenses that best fit your example:

* **{% resource theories-of-learning/tl-biological %}Biological{% endresource %}:** What is happening in the body or brain?
* **{% resource theories-of-learning/tl-constructivism %}Psychological{% endresource %}:** How do feedback, motivation, memory, or prior understanding shape what happens?
* **{% resource theories-of-learning/tl-sociocultural %}Sociocultural{% endresource %}:** How do other people, community, or culture shape what happens?
* **{% resource theories-of-learning/tl-sociopolitical %}Socio-political{% endresource %}:** How do power, rules, or institutions shape what happens?

Apply each lens to your example. Do not just define the lens.

## Make a 4-Slide Deck

Keep each slide concise. Use short phrases or 1–2 sentences per prompt.

{: .box }
**Slide 1 – Your example**

* Name and briefly describe the example.
* Include one image or visual.

{: .box }
**Slide 2 – Lens 1**

* What does this lens help explain?
* What does it leave out or explain less well?

{: .box }
**Slide 3 – Lens 2**

* What does this lens help explain?
* What does it leave out or explain less well?

{: .box }
**Slide 4 – Ethics and reflection**

* Which lens changed your understanding of the example most? Explain the change.
* **Ethical task:** Identify one person or group affected by this situation. What value matters to them (for example, fairness, autonomy, safety, belonging, or privacy)? What is one thing a person, school, employer, or platform should do differently to better protect that value?
* What question does this raise about whether an AI system could face a similar problem?
