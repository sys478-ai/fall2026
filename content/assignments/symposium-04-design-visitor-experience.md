**Part 4 of 5** on the way to the AI & Society Symposium (Nov. 17). Team deliverable, 10 points.

## What You're Doing

Translate your research into something a visitor can understand and engage with in approximately **five minutes**. Design it for the audience you chose in your proposal.

## What To Submit

1. **An exhibit storyboard** showing what visitors will see, do, and learn.
1. **A working or low-fidelity prototype** of your interactive element.
1. **Draft explanations** of each of the four exhibit sections:
   - **What You See:** an accessible introduction to the system and its purpose.
   - **What's Underneath:** how it works, its design choices, and whose interests shape it.
   - **What's at Stake:** evidence of its societal impacts, including your case study.
   - **What Could Be Different:** alternative designs, policies, or approaches.
1. **A plan for how you will communicate your evidence and recommendations** to visitors.

Submit one package per team on Canvas by the due date shown above.

### Tips
- The interactive element should make a visitor **decide something** or **notice something**, then connect it to your evidence.
- If you use images, music, or other people's work, make sure you have the right to.
- Every team member must be prepared to explain the research and answer questions.

## Next Class
During the next class, you will test your interactive experience with classmates who have not seen it. Watch what they do, not just what they say. Be ready to walk someone through the exhibit and find out whether its main message is coming across.
