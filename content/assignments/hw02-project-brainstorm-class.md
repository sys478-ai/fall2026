## Brainstorming Discussion for Symposium (11/17)
For this discussion, propose one idea you might be interested in exploring for the Fall Symposium.

Briefly address:

1. **Topic:** What AI system, controversy, or question interests you?
1. **Goal:** What should visitors understand, question, or experience?
1. **Interaction:** What could visitors actually do – not just read?
1. **Connection:** Is there an older technology, controversy, or social issue that this reminds you of? How is what's happening with AI now similar and different than what has come before? 

You do not need a fully developed idea. The goal is to generate possibilities we can discuss, combine, and develop as a class.

After posting, reply to at least one classmate with a question, connection, or idea that could help develop their proposal.
