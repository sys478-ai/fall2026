**Part 1 of 5** on the way to the AI & Society Symposium (Nov. 17). Team deliverable, 5 points.

## What You're Doing

Choose **one specific AI system** related to [your group's topic](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RO0D4q9kl8DMpCRJ68HVNG8dENCveRFA9v198ugvguI/edit?tab=t.8tn3r7daxvms). This should be a product or a specific deployment context, not a broad topic like "AI and Copyright." Decide on the central question your exhibit will investigate.

Your investigation should begin with a specific system, but ultimately help visitors understand something larger about AI and society. As you choose your system, consider: **What might this be a case of?** What broader questions about human behavior, social values, power, or institutions could it help us explore?

Your question does not have to be final. Teams are expected to revise it as they encounter evidence.

## What To Submit

A one-page team proposal that includes:

1. **The system and how people use it.**
2. **Your central research question and why it matters.**
3. **Two or three questions you'll need to investigate** to answer it.
4. **Your initial ideas about what visitors should learn or reconsider.** 
   What broader issue or question might your system help illuminate?
5. **Your audience.** Who do you most want to reach with your exhibit, and what do you hope they will understand, question, or do differently after visiting it?
   - An educator?
   - A parent?
   - A fellow student?
   - A child?
   - A policymaker?

Submit one proposal per team on Canvas by the due date shown above.

### Tips
- A strong question is specific, answerable with evidence, and about **why** and **who decides**, not just "is it good or bad?"
- ou don't need to know yet what your case reveals about the larger issue. That is something you'll discover through your investigation.
- Your audience shapes everything later: a parent, a college student who uses ChatGPT daily, and an educator deciding on classroom policy would each need a different exhibit.
- Your [team handout](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RO0D4q9kl8DMpCRJ68HVNG8dENCveRFA9v198ugvguI/edit?tab=t.8tn3r7daxvms) lists systems you might consider and questions to get started.

## Next Class

You will pitch your system and question to another group during the next class, and then revise based on what confused them. Be ready to explain your project in **60 seconds**, including what you're investigating and why someone should care.
