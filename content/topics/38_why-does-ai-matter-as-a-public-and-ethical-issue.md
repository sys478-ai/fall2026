## Topic / Focus

We open the course by asking why AI counts as a public and ethical issue, not only a technical one. You will consider what AI is supposed to help us do, and where it is already mediating decisions in your everyday and institutional life.

_Format: Full class · Lesson template: Discussion / Framing_

## Guiding Questions

- Why is AI a public and ethical issue, and not only a technical one?
- What is AI supposed to help us do?
- Where is AI already shaping the world around us?

## In This Class

In this meeting, we will:

- introduce the course's guiding questions as analytical tools
- analyze familiar claims about AI as descriptive, normative, or political
- survey examples of AI already operating in public and institutional life
- orient to the Field Guide's five lenses: deployment patterns, STS concepts, examples, ethics, and technical explainers

## Career / BRAID Integration

Today is an opening framing session on technology, society, and futures. There is no dedicated career module today; the framing sets up the career and course bridges that begin next week.
