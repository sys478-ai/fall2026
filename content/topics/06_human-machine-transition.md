## Topic / Focus

This class consolidates Module 1 before we move into machines. Rather than introducing another major concept, we review the biological, psychological, social/political, and moral lenses by applying them to the same human phenomena and asking what each lens makes visible, what it leaves out, and what students now have a more stable method for doing when they encounter an unfamiliar AI system later in the semester.

There is no major new reading for this class. Students should bring notes from the first five class meetings and be ready to reuse them.

## Guiding Questions

- What has each Module 1 lens helped us see about human learning, judgment, and action?
- What does each lens leave out?
- Why is asking "Which lens is best?" usually the wrong question?
- How can these same habits prepare us to analyze AI systems in Module 2?
