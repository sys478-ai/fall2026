## Topic / Focus

This transfer lab asks you to apply the domain framework on your own: choose your own Example in the Wild, identify its domain, name 2-3 deployment patterns, offer an STS explanation, and apply an ethical lens, without the scaffolding of the previous four meetings.

_Format: Full class · Lesson template: Transfer / Comparative Lab_

## Guiding Questions

- Which domain and example did you choose, and why does it matter to you?
- Which 2-3 deployment patterns show up most clearly in your example?
- What STS explanation and ethical lens best fit your case?

## In This Class

In this meeting, we will:

- choose an Example in the Wild and identify its domain independently
- name 2-3 deployment patterns present in the example
- apply an STS explanation and an ethical lens to the case
