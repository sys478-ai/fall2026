## Topic / Focus

We close this unit by asking what futures technologies promise, and who gets to define what counts as a desirable future. A career bridge on three possible lives and SMART goals connects your own future-imagining to the course's account of sociotechnical imaginaries.

_Format: Career ~50-60 min + integrated course ~40-50 min · Lesson template: Career + Course Bridge_

## Guiding Questions

- What futures do AI systems promise, implicitly or explicitly?
- Who gets to define what counts as a desirable future?
- How are futures imagined and shaped, rather than simply predicted?
