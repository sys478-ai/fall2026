## Topic / Focus

Today asks what judgment, ethics, and theory of mind add to intelligence, and what a machine can imitate versus what it might lack. We use this as a seed for the BRAID question of what assumptions we make when we call something intelligent.

## Guiding Questions

- What is theory of mind, and can a machine have it?
- What can a system imitate convincingly without possessing judgment or ethics?
- What assumptions do we make when we call a system 'intelligent'?
