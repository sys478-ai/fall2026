## What This Explains

AI did not arrive all at once. The field grew through a long sequence of technical bets, public promises, winters, breakthroughs, and institutional choices. A timeline helps you see **which ideas got funded**, **which demos shaped public imagination**, and **when governance started lagging behind capability**.

## How To Use It

Use the [interactive History of AI timeline](/field-guide/ai-history) to move from early computing and the Turing Test through neural networks, deep learning, and large language models. Each entry can connect to field guide recognition cards so you can ask: what pattern showed up here, and who had the power to set the terms?

## Critical Bridge

When you scan the chronology, notice what is missing as carefully as what is celebrated. Contested claims, failed promises, and deployment harms are part of the history too – not side notes after the technical milestones.
