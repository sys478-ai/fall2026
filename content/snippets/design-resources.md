## Poster Templates and Design Resources

**Poster size:** 48 inches wide × 36 inches tall (landscape). You may customize any template, but the poster must keep the required analytical content: system, argument, evidence, societal implications, questions and possibilities, and references.

### 36 × 48-Inch Poster Templates

- [UNC Asheville CrAFT Studio – Poster Design Resources](https://library.unca.edu/craftstudio/lfp_design) – Templates for PowerPoint, Google Slides, Canva, and Keynote, including 36 × 48-inch formats.
- [PosterScientist – Free Research Poster Templates](https://www.posterscientist.com/templates) – Customizable research poster layouts, including 36 × 48-inch options.
- [Genigraphics – PowerPoint Poster Templates](https://www.genigraphics.com/templates) – Free editable PowerPoint templates in multiple sizes.
- [PosterPresentations – Free Poster Templates](https://www.posterpresentations.com/free-poster-templates.html) – Various layouts and sizes, including 36 × 48 inches.

### Additional Templates and Inspiration

- [PosterNerd – Billboard Poster Templates](https://www.posternerd.com/billboard-poster-templates) – Argument-centered posters featuring a prominent takeaway supported by research.
- [Canva – Research Poster Templates](https://www.canva.com/posters/templates/research/) – Visually engaging, customizable templates. Check the dimensions and resize to 48 × 36 inches.
- [Derek Crowe – Butter Poster](https://derekcrowe.net/butterposter) – A research poster layout emphasizing clear communication and supporting evidence.
- [Colin Purrington – Research Poster Design and Templates](https://colinpurrington.com/tips/poster-design/) – Free templates and practical advice on organizing research posters.
