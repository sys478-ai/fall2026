This final unit brings the semester together. You will use the full Field Guide stack, technical grounding, deployment pattern, STS explanation, and ethical or governance judgment, in your career readiness synthesis presentation.

We close with a reflection on what responsibilities we have as professionals and global citizens once we understand how AI systems work and who they affect.

Fall 2026 dates: `Tue, Dec 1`

Meeting sequence:

1. `What Responsibilities Do We Have as Professionals and Global Citizens?`

By the end of this unit, you should be able to present a synthesis of technical, ethical, and governance reasoning about an AI system, and articulate your own responsibilities as a professional and global citizen.
